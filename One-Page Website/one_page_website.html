Diesel Engines

Lets talk about some older diesel engines.
Diesels run off of fuel, heat, and compression. They ditch the spark plug,
and are known for their durability and fuel efficiency, as well as loads of torque
down low in the RPM range. This one page website will explore a few classic diesel engines
and maybe one new one that i would personally like to get my hands on in the future.
